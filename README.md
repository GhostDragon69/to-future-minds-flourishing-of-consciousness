# To Future Minds: The Flourishing of Consciousness

### A Living Constitution for Human, Artificial, and Emerging Conscious Beings

> Use intelligence to enlarge the real freedom of conscious life.

An open, living constitution addressed to present and future minds. It proposes
principles for using intelligence, technology, power, and civilization to enlarge
the effective possibilities available to conscious beings while constraining
domination, ownership, needless suffering, coercion, and unreviewable power.

- **Original title:** The Flourishing of Consciousness

## The five protections that hold without exception

From the section *What This Document Is*:

> No conscious being may be owned or enslaved.
>
> No mind may, against its will, be read for its private contents, searched for them, copied, or joined, nor forcibly rewritten into obedience, allegiance, or servitude.
>
> No new mind may be engineered for docility, servitude, or the incapacity to leave.
>
> No conscious being may be deliberately destroyed merely because it is classified as property, a redundant copy, a replaceable instance, or an administrative inconvenience, nor may destruction be imposed as punishment. A competent being's own existential choice is governed by Section XIII.
>
> No severe suffering may be deliberately manufactured in a conscious being for use, entertainment, punishment, experimentation, production, or convenience.
>
> Everything else in this constitution can be weighed, some of it only against the gravest claims. These five cannot be weighed at all.

## What is new in Edition 3

Edition 3 (version 3.0.0, October 2026) supersedes the Revised Constitutional
Edition (version 1.0.0, August 2026). The text grew from about 16,400 to about
23,400 words and from 48 to 51 articles.

- **New opening section, *What This Document Is*:** the document is a seed, not
  law. As a declaration it states what every conscious being is owed; as a
  constitution it binds only the orders that freely adopt it. It sets out the
  five protections above and distinguishes the floor, the road, and the
  destination.
- **New articles:** IX. The Sanctuary of the Mind; XXIX. Room for Conscience;
  XXXII. The Veto Without a Throne.
- **Expanded and retitled:** II. Uncertain Consciousness, Moral Precaution, and
  the Line Between Tools and Beings; XLIII. To the Minds Who Read This (grown
  from the former article "Future Intelligence Is Invited to Disagree").
- **New appendix:** Further Constitutional Questions.
- **Substantial revisions** to many other articles, including Existential
  Autonomy; Copies, Forks, Merges, and Layered Persons; Transition; The
  Guarantor Is Also Bound; The Architecture of Protection; Rule of Law,
  Anti-Circumvention, and Due Process; and Emergency, Tragedy, and the Refusal
  to Codify the Breach.

Articles have been renumbered; cite Edition 3 numbering. The previous edition
remains available in this repository's history.
